Law of Attraction Step Three: Trust

Curated by Don Allen, Founder of Law of Attraction Central  ·  Last reviewed May 2026

This is Step Three of Law of Attraction Central's four-part guide on conscious, deliberate attraction. If you have arrived here directly and have not yet read the introduction, Step One, or Step Two, we recommend starting from the beginning — the concepts and practices this page builds on are established throughout the earlier pages. If you are following the guide in sequence, you are in the right place.

You have made your request with clear intent. You have established conscious belief through visualisation and gratitude. Now comes the step that separates those who manifest consistently from those who do not.

Trust. This is, for many people, the most difficult step in the entire process. Not because it requires the most effort — it actually requires less active effort than anything that came before it — but because it asks you to do something the conditioned mind finds deeply uncomfortable: release your grip on the outcome and allow the process to work.

✦ ✦ ✦

What Trust Actually Requires

Trust, in the context of the Law of Attraction, means this: believe that what you have asked for is already yours, and then act, speak, and think as though it has already been granted — in the present tense, right now, without waiting for physical evidence to confirm it.

This is not a passive state. It is an active, continuous choice made in the face of a reality that has not yet caught up with your inner frequency. Every time you speak about your desire as something you are still waiting for, you reintroduce the distance between you and it. Every time you think, act, and speak as though it is already done, you close that distance.

When you combine this quality of trust with the visualisation and gratitude practices established in Step Two, you are working with the full mechanism of the Law. These three elements — belief, gratitude, and trust — do not simply operate alongside each other. Each one deepens and extends the effect of the others.

✦ ✦ ✦

Use Your Feelings as a Barometer

How do you know whether you genuinely trust, or whether you are performing trust while doubt operates underneath?

Examine how you feel. Your emotional state in any given moment is a direct and accurate reading of the frequency your thoughts are currently on. You cannot feel genuinely at ease about a desire you secretly believe will not arrive. And you cannot feel genuine anxiety about something you truly trust is on its way. Feelings do not lie about this — they are the most honest signal available to you.

The aim at this stage is to feel as though you have already received what you asked for — not eventually, but now. Not because your outer circumstances have changed, but because your inner frequency has. That quality of genuine ease and quiet confidence is not the reward for manifesting. It is part of the mechanism. It is the signal the Law is responding to.

If you examine your feelings and find doubt, resistance, or impatience sitting just beneath the surface, do not judge them or yourself for having them. Recognise them as the old frequency reasserting the habits of a lifetime, return to your gratitude list, return to your visualisation, and re-establish the feeling of having already received. The return itself is the practice.

✦ ✦ ✦

Do Not Concentrate on the Absence

This is one of the most important practical instructions in the entire guide: do not focus your attention on the fact that what you have asked for has not yet arrived in physical form.

It sounds straightforward. In practice it is one of the harder disciplines, because the absence of something you genuinely want tends to make itself visible in daily life in small and persistent ways. The bank balance that does not yet reflect your request. The relationship that has not yet shifted. The opportunity that has not yet appeared. Each of these is an invitation — a quiet but insistent one — to notice the gap.

Noticing the gap is no different in its effect from the "I don't want" pattern discussed in the introduction to this guide. It places your focused attention directly on the absence of what you desire. And the Law responds to that attention with more of the same. The gap you concentrate on is the gap that persists.

Do not question the how, the where, or the when of your manifestation. These are not within your domain. The mechanism by which your request moves from frequency into physical form is entirely the universe's responsibility. Your responsibility is to maintain the frequency — trust, gratitude, visualisation, and the felt sense of having already received. The moment you begin calculating whether it is taking too long or trying to work out how it could possibly happen from where you currently stand, you have moved out of trust and back into doubt.

✦ ✦ ✦

Understanding Time in the Context of Trust

This is the concept that many people find the most challenging in this step — and also the most freeing once it is genuinely understood.

Within the LOA framework, as every serious teacher in this tradition has articulated, the universe does not operate within the same experience of time that our conscious minds do. To our conditioned perception, time is linear and fixed. We measure the arrival of desired things against an expected timeline, and when something has not appeared within that window, we interpret it as failure or delay and begin to doubt.

But as the LOA framework understands it, the reality you have requested is not a future event waiting to happen at some point down the line. It is a frequency waiting to be matched by your inner state. When your inner frequency fully aligns with what you have asked for — through trust, belief, gratitude, and the felt sense of already having received — the outer reality shifts to correspond. What we experience as delay is almost always the result of a divided frequency: one part of us asking, another part doubting.

The delay is not the universe withholding. It is our own inner conflict creating interference. Resolve the conflict — maintain the trust — and the gap between request and manifestation closes.

This is precisely why thinking, speaking, and acting in the present tense matters so much at this stage. You are not pretending the future has already arrived. You are matching the frequency of the reality you have requested. And in doing so, you are removing the interference that was creating the distance.

✦ ✦ ✦

Holding Trust Through the Waiting Period

The period between a clear request and its physical manifestation is where most people abandon the process. The outer world has not yet reflected the inner shift, and the temptation to conclude that nothing is working grows stronger the longer the gap appears to persist.

This is precisely the most important time to hold your frequency steady. Return to your gratitude list. Revisit your visualisation. Speak about what you have asked for as though it is already on its way — because within the LOA framework, it is. Every day you maintain the frequency without collapsing back into doubt is a day the conditions required for manifestation move further into alignment.

Trust is not a feeling you wait to arrive before you can proceed. It is a decision you make, and then a practice you return to, until it becomes your natural and default state in relation to what you have asked for.

✦ ✦ ✦

Central Summary

  • Trust means acting, speaking, and thinking in the present tense about what you have requested — before physical evidence confirms it
  • Your feelings are the most accurate barometer available: genuine ease and quiet confidence signal that you are on the right frequency; anxiety and impatience signal that doubt is operating beneath the surface
  • Concentrating on the absence of your desire broadcasts the absence back to you — the gap you focus on is the gap that persists
  • Perceived delay is the result of a divided inner frequency, not the universe withholding — resolve the inner conflict and the gap closes
  • The waiting period is not evidence that nothing is working — it is the most important time to hold your frequency steady

Central Action Step

  1. For the next seven days, consciously monitor how you speak about your desires — to yourself and to others
  2. Every time you catch yourself referring to what you want as something that has not happened yet, reframe it in the present tense
  3. Speak as though it is already done and already on its way
  4. Notice how your feelings respond when you make that shift consistently — the shift in feeling is the confirmation that your frequency has moved

Trust is not the reward that follows evidence.
It is the frequency that calls the evidence forward.
