Law of Attraction Step Two: Conscious Belief

Curated by Don Allen, Founder of Law of Attraction Central  ·  Last reviewed May 2026

This is Step Two of Law of Attraction Central's four-part guide on conscious, deliberate attraction. If you have arrived here directly and have not yet read the introduction or Step One, we recommend starting there — the concepts this page builds on are established in both. If you are following the guide in sequence, you are in the right place.

Now that you have made your request with clear intent and genuine expectation, it is time to take the second step. If Step One was about direction — deciding what you want and stating it clearly — Step Two is about identity. It is about who you are in relation to what you have asked for.

The essence of this step is deceptively simple: consciously accept that what you have asked for is already yours. Believe that your request was granted the moment it was made.

✦ ✦ ✦

Why This Step Is Where Most People Stall

For many people, this is the point at which the process breaks down. Accepting something as already received — before there is any physical evidence of it — can feel like pretending. Like self-deception dressed up in positive language. Some dismiss it entirely. Others go through the motions without genuinely feeling it, and then wonder why nothing shifts.

The resistance is understandable. Most of us were conditioned from an early age to trust only what we can see, measure, and verify. Believing in something that has not yet appeared in physical form runs directly against that conditioning. This is precisely why the reconditioning process introduced earlier in this guide is not a preliminary step — it is an ongoing one.

Doubt is not a neutral force. It is an active one. The moment doubt enters your belief about a request, it introduces a conflicting signal into the frequency you are broadcasting. You are simultaneously asking for something and transmitting the vibration of not having it. The Law responds to both and the result is delay, inconsistency, or nothing at all.

Conscious belief is not make-believe. It is the deliberate decision to hold a single, uncontradicted frequency about what you have requested — and to feel that frequency as fully and as consistently as possible.

✦ ✦ ✦

Visualisation: The Tool That Makes Belief Tangible

The most powerful way to establish and maintain conscious belief is through visualisation. This is not a passive or vague imagining. It is an active, sensory, fully inhabited experience of already having what you have requested — conducted with the same emotional reality as a lived moment.

You must begin to feel what you will feel when your request manifests — not later, when evidence arrives, but now. The subconscious mind, which is the instrument through which the Law ultimately operates, does not distinguish cleanly between a vividly imagined experience and a real one. When you visualise with genuine feeling and sensory specificity, you are generating a real frequency. A real transmission. One the Law can act on.

When visualising, engage all five senses. This is not optional — it is what separates a visualisation that produces results from one that does not. Sight alone is not enough. Sound, smell, touch, and taste belong in the scene wherever they naturally fit.

A practical example. Suppose your request is to earn a specific amount of money. Rather than simply picturing a number, visualise what your daily life looks like when that income is your reality. What are you doing with it? Perhaps you are buying a meaningful gift for someone you care about. Visualise the setting — sitting across from them, somewhere comfortable and familiar. See the expression on their face as they receive it. Hear their laughter. Feel the warmth of being genuinely generous with someone you love. Imagine the gift itself — its weight, its texture, how it looks in their hands. Let the happiness of that moment be real to you right now, in this room, in this chair. There are genuinely no limits to how far this process can take you when you engage it fully and without reservation.

The more specific and emotionally alive your visualisation, the more powerful the frequency you generate. Vague imaginings produce vague results. A fully inhabited, sensorially rich visualisation is a direct and deliberate transmission.

✦ ✦ ✦

Gratitude as a Belief Amplifier

Alongside visualisation, gratitude is one of the most important feelings you can bring to this step — and it works on two distinct levels simultaneously.

The first is gratitude felt in advance. Thank the universe for what you have requested before it arrives. This is not a performance or a formality. It is a frequency that contains within it the assumption of receipt. Genuine gratitude for something not yet received is only possible when you truly believe it is coming. That belief, expressed as gratitude, is among the most powerful signals you can emit.

The second is gratitude for what you have already received. This is equally important and far more commonly overlooked. Look honestly at your life right now and find what is genuinely good in it. The people who care about you. The resources available to you. The capacity for growth you are demonstrating simply by being here and doing this work. Write these things down. Return to the list regularly. Add to it. This practice establishes a positive frequency before you have even begun a new visualisation session, and it builds on itself over time.

These two forms of gratitude work together in a specific way. Gratitude for what you already have raises your frequency to a state from which conscious belief becomes more natural and more sustainable. Gratitude for what you have requested amplifies the frequency of the request itself. Begin each session of belief work by reading your existing gratitude list first. Let it do its work. Then move into your visualisation from that elevated starting point.

✦ ✦ ✦

Maintaining the Belief

Conscious belief is not a single event. It is a practice maintained between the moment of your request and the moment of its manifestation — however long that takes.

Every time doubt surfaces — and it will — recognise it for what it is: the old frequency attempting to reassert itself out of long habit. Do not fight it or criticise yourself for it. Simply return to your visualisation. Return to your gratitude. Return to the feeling of already having what you have asked for. The return itself is the practice.

The Law of Attraction responds to the frequency you hold most consistently over time. Make conscious belief your dominant frequency and the conditions required for your request to manifest will continue to move toward you, whether or not you can see that movement yet.

✦ ✦ ✦

Central Summary

  • Conscious belief means accepting that what you have requested is already yours — before physical evidence confirms it
  • Doubt is an active conflicting signal that delays or prevents manifestation, not a neutral state to be tolerated
  • Visualisation is the primary practical tool: engage all five senses, inhabit the scene fully, and generate the emotional frequency of already having what you have asked for
  • Gratitude works on two levels — felt in advance for what is coming, and felt genuinely for what you already have
  • Both forms of gratitude raise your frequency and together they multiply the power of everything else in this process

Central Action Step

  1. Write a gratitude list of the genuinely good things already present in your life — begin with the most fundamental and build outward
  2. Read this list before every visualisation session — let it establish your frequency before you begin
  3. Add to the list regularly — it is not a one-time exercise but the foundation from which your conscious belief operates
  4. After reading the list, move directly into a visualisation of your request, engaging all five senses and holding the feeling of already having what you have asked for

Belief is not the reward that follows evidence.
It is the signal that calls the evidence forward.
